Rough & Tumble

2026 / GAME

In Rough & Tumble you transform into a golf ball, and move objects as a human to launch yourself to new places. It's a game about wandering the woods and trying stuff out. It's a game about the endless summer holidays. It's a game about finding your way and staying silly.

Play Rough & Tumble!!! (for free!) Try the trailer Digest the devlog Seek the sountrack

Rough & Tumble is packed full of physics objects, and they're all useful in their own way. The mushroom is weak but reliable; the fan is handy for changing your trajectory. The conveyor belt does its best work when you're already moving.

It's an open-world problem-solving sand-box marble-run platformer.

Go play it! It's fun!
