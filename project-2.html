Lazer Curling!!!

2023 / GAME

Lazer Curling is a game where you and a friend play as curling pucks and shoot each other with lazers. It's chaotic, flailing, and hilarious.

Play Lazer Curling Here (for free!)

In between each round, you both get to choose upgrades. You can freeze your opponent in place, or teleport across the rink. You'll need to constantly change your strategy to counter your foe.

I believe that laser should be spelled with a Z. It has all the energy of a word with a Z, and its lack of a Z is admonishable. Luckily each of us has the power to carry out the change we want to see in the world. This is the hill I'll die on.
